Frequently asked questions

How does the free trial work?
Your listing goes live immediately. You won't be charged anything for the first 30 days. After that, your chosen plan begins. You can cancel any time before the trial ends with no charge.
Can I cancel at any time?
Yes. Monthly plans can be cancelled at any time — your listing stays live until the end of that billing period. Annual plans are refunded pro-rata if cancelled within the first 60 days.
Do you handle payments or bookings?
SF·Kids is a discovery and referral platform — parents click through directly to your website or booking system to complete their enrollment and payment. You keep 100% of what families pay you.
What is the Featured badge?
Featured listings appear at the top of all relevant search results, with a gold ⭐ Featured badge on your activity card. In our data, featured listings get 3–5× more click-throughs than standard listings.
What information do I need to submit?
Activity name, short description, category, age range, price, neighborhood, schedule, and your website or booking URL. The whole form takes about 3 minutes.
Can I list multiple activities?
Yes. Each activity is a separate listing. Multi-activity discounts are available — email hello@sf-kids.com for bundle pricing.
