Presidio Youth Soccer Camp

🏫 Bay Area Youth Athletics | View provider →
★★★★★ 4.9 (128 reviews) | ✓ 400+ bookings

Join the most popular youth soccer program in San Francisco! Our certified coaches run skill-building sessions at the beautiful Presidio fields every Saturday and Sunday. Whether your child is a total beginner or a budding player, we group kids by skill level so everyone gets the right challenge. We focus on fun, teamwork, and building a lifelong love of the game.

Sessions include drills, small-sided games, and a cool-down stretch. All coaches are US Soccer certified and background-checked. Cleats recommended but not required.

What's included

  • Certified, background-checked coach (1:8 ratio)
  • All training equipment and pinnies
  • Skill assessment and group placement
  • Post-session progress card
  • Water break and snack (please bring a water bottle)

What to bring

  • Water bottle
  • Athletic clothes
  • Cleats (sneakers are fine)
  • Sunscreen
